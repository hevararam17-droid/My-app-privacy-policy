Privacy Policy for Kaniba Milk Center Last updated: September 24, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Kaniba Milk Center, developed by Hevar Khalid, collects, uses, stores, and protects customer information when you use our baby shop application. We respect your privacy and collect only the information necessary to process and deliver your orders. 1. Information We Collect When you place an order through Kaniba Milk Center, we may collect: • Name — to identify the customer and manage the order. • Phone number — to contact the customer regarding order confirmation and delivery. • Delivery address — to deliver the ordered products to the requested location. 2. How We Use Your Information Your information is used only for purposes related to your order, including: • Processing and managing customer orders. • Confirming orders and communicating with customers. • Arranging and completing product deliveries. • Contacting customers about issues related to their orders. • Providing customer support. We do not sell customers' personal information. 3. Sharing of Information We do not share customers' personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. To fulfill an order, we may share the necessary customer information with delivery personnel or delivery service providers responsible for delivering the order. Information shared for delivery purposes is intended only to fulfill the order and provide the requested service. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or a lawful legal request. 4. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect customer information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. 5. Data Retention We retain customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary to process orders, provide customer support, maintain business records, and comply with applicable legal requirements. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it. 6. Data Deletion Requests Customers may request deletion of their personal information and associated order information by contacting us at the email address below. Please include your name, phone number, and a description of your request. We will review and process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period. Certain information may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes, such as resolving disputes or maintaining legally required records. 7. Children's Privacy Kaniba Milk Center is a shopping application for purchasing baby and children's products. It is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. Customers should place orders through their parents, guardians, or other responsible adults where applicable. 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or data-handling practices. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. 9.
